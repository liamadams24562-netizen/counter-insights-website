Why me

Hands-on in both sectors – not just spreadsheets.

I’ve run shifts and led teams on the shop floor and behind the counter in both sectors I know best – fuel retail and hospitality – so I understand stock, waste, rotas and margins from the inside.

Forecourts

I’ve managed petrol stations and worked as a Business and Systems Analyst at Engen, a fuel company, where I reviewed financial, sales and product records to find ways to reduce costs and improve profit.

Hospitality

I’ve supervised teams at more than one London restaurant, including Haute Dolci and Heavenly Desserts – dessert, coffee and brunch restaurants. I currently work as a restaurant supervisor alongside running Counter Insights.

I know what a busy service feels like, how quickly stock moves and what happens when the rota doesn’t match the rush.

The two have a lot in common: stock checked against sales, deliveries to verify, peaks to staff for and prices set with one eye on the competition. I combine that hands-on view with five years as a data analyst, using Python, SQL and databases, Excel, Tableau and Power BI, plus data compliance (including GDPR) and information security risk.

I am a PECB Certified Data Protection Officer. Your figures stay yours. How I handle them is set out in the privacy notice.
