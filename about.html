Why me

I’ve worked the shifts, not only the spreadsheets.

I’ve run shifts and led teams on the shop floor and behind the counter, in fuel retail and in hospitality. Stock, waste, rotas and margins are familiar from the inside, not only from a file.

Forecourts

I’ve managed petrol stations. I also worked as a Business and Systems Analyst at Engen, a fuel company, reviewing financial, sales and product records to find ways to cut costs and improve profit.

Hospitality

I’ve supervised teams at more than one London restaurant, including Haute Dolci and Heavenly Desserts. Those are dessert, coffee and brunch restaurants. I still work as a restaurant supervisor alongside Counter Insights.

I know what a busy service feels like, how fast stock moves, and what happens when the rota misses the rush.

The two worlds share a lot: stock checked against sales, deliveries to verify, peaks to staff for, and prices set with an eye on the competition. Alongside that, I have spent five years as a data analyst, using Python, SQL and databases, Excel, Tableau and Power BI, plus data compliance (including GDPR) and information security risk.

I am a PECB Certified Data Protection Officer. Your figures stay yours. The privacy notice sets out how I handle them.
