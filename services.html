What I do

Seven services, two kinds of counter.

The same questions matter whether you sell litres or lattes. Here is what each service looks like on a forecourt and in a restaurant or café. Most clients start with a Business Health Check, which covers several of these in one go.

Restaurants and cafés Petrol stations Other small businesses Seven services Packages

Restaurants and cafés

Where food cost and labour decide the margin.

Independent restaurants, cafés and dessert or brunch spots. I use the till, stock and rota data you already have to show food cost, which dishes earn their place, how your prices sit against nearby places, and whether the rota matches the covers.

  • Food cost % and waste
  • Menu profitability
  • Prices against local competitors
  • Rotas that match covers

If you use Apicbase, see Apicbase support.

Forecourts and petrol stations

Fuel, shop and car wash, each pulling their weight.

Independent and dealer-run sites. I look at fuel deliveries against pump sales, pump and shop margins, prices against nearby sites, and staffing for peak hours.

  • Fuel deliveries against pump sales
  • Pump and shop margins
  • Prices against nearby sites
  • Staffing for peak hours

Other small businesses

Convenience stores and independent shops.

If your business records sales, stock or bookings, I can probably help. For a shop, that usually means stock checked against what you sold, which lines earn their shelf space, prices against nearby stores, and staffing for the hours customers actually come in.

The seven services

What each one looks like in practice.

Stock and waste tracking

What you bought against what you sold – and where the gap comes from.

On the forecourt and in the shop

Fuel deliveries against pump sales, alongside (not replacing) your wetstock monitoring. Shop lines checked for waste, out-of-date stock and shrinkage.

In the restaurant or café

Food purchases against dishes sold to show your real food cost %. Spot over-portioning, spoilage and stock that goes missing.

Margins and what earns its place

See profit, not just turnover – line by line.

On the forecourt and in the shop

Pump margin against volume, and which shop categories and lines really earn their shelf space.

In the restaurant or café

Menu profitability in plain words: winners, popular but thin, hidden gems and problem dishes – each with a clear next step.

Pricing vs local competitors

Price with confidence, not guesswork.

On the forecourt and in the shop

Your pump prices against nearby sites using public price data, and key shop prices against local convenience stores.

In the restaurant or café

Plate cost against menu price, and your prices against comparable restaurants and cafés nearby.

Busy hours and rotas

Match your staffing to when customers actually come in.

On the forecourt and in the shop

Transactions by hour and day across pumps, shop and car wash – set against the hours you have staff on.

In the restaurant or café

Covers and sales by hour and day against staffed hours – where you are stretched at peaks and overstaffed when it’s quiet.

Supplier and delivery checks

Pay what you agreed, and get what you paid for.

On the forecourt and in the shop

Fuel delivery timing and variance trends; shop supplier invoices against agreed prices, with short deliveries flagged.

In the restaurant or café

Ingredient price creep, short deliveries and substitutions – plus how suppliers compare on your key lines.

Forecasting

Plan ahead with realistic numbers, all assumptions shown.

On the forecourt and in the shop

Litres and shop sales for the weeks ahead, to time fuel orders and reduce the risk of run-outs.

In the restaurant or café

Covers for the weeks ahead, so ordering and the rota match likely demand.

Simple dashboards

Your key numbers in one place, refreshed monthly with a short “what’s changed” note.

On the forecourt and in the shop

Litres, pump margin, shop sales and car wash – side by side.

In the restaurant or café

Sales, covers, average spend, food cost % and labour hours.

What I work with

No new software needed.

Till/EPOS and back-office reports, supplier invoices and delivery notes, rotas or timesheets, and booking, loyalty or delivery-platform reports where you have them. Summary data only – never card numbers.
