Restaurants and cafés

Where food cost and the rota decide the week.

For independent restaurants, cafés, and dessert or brunch places. I use the till, stock and rota data you already keep, so you can see food cost, which dishes earn a place, how prices sit against nearby sites, and whether the rota follows the covers.

  • Food cost % and waste
  • Menu profitability
  • Prices against local competitors
  • Rotas that match covers

Pumps, shop and car wash, each earning their place.

Independent and dealer-run sites. I compare fuel deliveries with pump sales, look at pump and shop margins, set prices against nearby sites, and check staffing for the busy hours.

  • Fuel deliveries against pump sales
  • Pump and shop margins
  • Prices against nearby sites
  • Staffing for peak hours

Fuel and Stock Loss Check-up

Every forecourt and shop loses some money to drive-offs, unpaid fuel and missing stock, but most owners only ever see the total. I go through your till, pump and stock records to show where the losses really are, and how to get more of them back.

  • Where the losses are: by product, pump, shift and time of day
  • Drive-offs and unpaid fuel: what’s being reported, recovered and missed
  • Your recovery process and evidence: simple templates and staff checklists
  • CCTV and number plate cameras done right: policies, signage and retention rules from a certified Data Protection Officer

You get a short plain-English report with the 3–5 changes that matter most, plus a free chat. I don’t chase debts or look up drivers myself, but I’ll help you compare recovery providers.

Other small businesses

Shops that already record sales or stock.

Convenience stores and other independents. If you record sales, stock or bookings, I can probably help. For a shop that usually means stock against what you sold, which lines deserve the shelf, prices against nearby shops, and staffing for the hours people actually come in.

The seven services

Same service, two counters.

Stock and waste tracking

Compare what you bought with what you sold, and see where the difference comes from.

On the forecourt and in the shop

Fuel deliveries set against pump sales. This sits alongside your wetstock monitoring. It does not replace it. Shop lines are checked for waste, stock that has gone out of date, and shrinkage.

In the restaurant or café

Food purchases set against dishes sold, so the food cost % is real. This is where over-portioning, spoilage and missing stock show up.

Margins and what earns its place

Look at profit, not only turnover, one line at a time.

On the forecourt and in the shop

Pump margin against volume, and which shop categories and lines really earn the shelf they take up.

In the restaurant or café

The menu in plain language: winners, dishes that sell but earn little, hidden gems, and problem dishes. Each one comes with a next step.

Pricing vs local competitors

Set prices from evidence, not a hunch.

On the forecourt and in the shop

Your pump prices against nearby sites, using public price data, and key shop prices against local convenience stores.

In the restaurant or café

What a plate costs against the menu price, and your prices against similar restaurants and cafés nearby.

Busy hours and rotas

Put people on shift when customers are actually there.

On the forecourt and in the shop

Transactions by hour and day across pumps, shop and car wash, set against the hours you have staff on.

In the restaurant or café

Covers and sales by hour and day against staffed hours. Where the peaks stretch you, and where it is quiet and you are overstaffed.

Supplier and delivery checks

Check you were charged what you agreed, and received what you paid for.

On the forecourt and in the shop

Fuel delivery timing and how the variance moves over time. Shop invoices checked against agreed prices, with short deliveries flagged.

In the restaurant or café

Ingredient prices creeping up, short deliveries and substitutions, and how suppliers compare on the lines that matter.

Forecasting

Plan the next few weeks with realistic numbers, and every assumption shown.

On the forecourt and in the shop

Litres and shop sales for the weeks ahead, so fuel orders are timed and run-outs are less likely.

In the restaurant or café

Covers for the weeks ahead, so the order and the rota follow the demand you are likely to see.

Simple dashboards

The numbers that matter, in one place, refreshed each month with a short note on what changed.

On the forecourt and in the shop

Litres, pump margin, shop sales and car wash, side by side.

In the restaurant or café

Sales, covers, average spend, food cost % and labour hours.
