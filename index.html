How it works

Three steps, then a plan.

1

Send a starting pack

A month is enough to begin. More history helps. Till or EPOS reports and supplier invoices. Ordinary exports. Nothing to install.

2

I work through the numbers

I match purchases to sales, work out margins, map the busy hours and check supplier prices. If I need to ask something, I’ll ask it in plain language.

3

You leave with priorities

A short written report, in order of what to do first, plus a call or a visit so the next steps are clear.
