For forecourts, restaurants, cafés and shops

Protect your margins behind the counter.

Practical data made simple for counter-service small businesses – built from the till reports and supplier invoices you already have. No new software, no jargon, just clear actions.

Your till, EPOS and supplier invoices record everything: every sale, every delivery, every busy hour. But the reports usually sit unused while you run the business, and big decisions still come down to gut feel. Large chains have analysts for this. Independents usually don’t.

That’s where I come in. I’m Liam Adams, a Wembley-based data consultant. I take the numbers you already collect and turn them into a short list of what to do next – where stock and waste are costing you, what really earns its place, and when you need more (or fewer) people on shift.

How it works

Three simple steps.

1

Share your data

A month of data to start, more history helps: till/EPOS reports and supplier invoices. Standard exports are fine – there is nothing new to buy or install.

2

I do the analysis

I reconcile purchases against sales, work out your margins, map your busy hours and check supplier prices – and ask you any questions in plain terms.

3

You get a clear action plan

A short written report with your priorities in order, and a walkthrough call or visit so you know exactly what to do next.
