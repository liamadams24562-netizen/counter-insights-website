Restaurants and cafés

Apicbase support.

If your business already uses Apicbase, I can support you with it. Apicbase is a stock and recipe management system. You do not need to buy anything new to work with me.

For businesses that already use it.

I help restaurants and cafés get more from the recipes, stock and purchasing information they already keep in Apicbase, alongside till or EPOS exports and supplier invoices.

That sits with the same questions I work on for other kitchens:

  • Food purchases and recipes set against dishes sold, so you can see a real food cost %
  • Where stock is short, wasted or going missing
  • Which dishes earn their place on the menu, and which do not

Tell me what you need. I will say honestly whether I can help, then quote for the work. There is no set price.

The same care applies to any personal data in those exports. I only ask for what the work needs, and I never take card numbers. The details are in the privacy notice.

Start with a free chat.

Mention Apicbase when you get in touch. If you would like a first look at the numbers, ask for the free margin check-up. It is a no-obligation conversation, not a full review.
