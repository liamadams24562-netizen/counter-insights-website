Restaurants and cafés

Already using Apicbase?

If your restaurant or café is on Apicbase, I can support you with it. It’s a stock and recipe system. You don’t need to buy anything else to work with me.

For kitchens that are already on it.

I help you get more from the recipes, stock and purchasing you already keep in Apicbase, next to your till or EPOS exports and supplier invoices.

That lines up with the same questions I ask in other kitchens:

  • Food purchases and recipes set against dishes sold, so the food cost % is a real one
  • Where stock is short, wasted, or going missing
  • Which dishes earn a place on the menu, and which don’t

Tell me what you need. I’ll say plainly whether I can help, then quote for the work. There isn’t a set price.

Personal data in those exports gets the same care. I only ask for what the work needs, and I never take card numbers. The details are in the privacy notice.

Mention it when you get in touch.

Say that you use Apicbase. If you want a first look at the numbers, ask for the free margin check-up. That’s a conversation, not a full review, and there’s no obligation.
