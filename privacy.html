Privacy notice.

How I handle personal data when you use this website, send the contact form, or we work together. Written in plain language.

Who I am

I am Liam Adams, a sole trader working as Counter Insights. I am the controller for the personal data described here. I am a PECB Certified Data Protection Officer, and I apply that standard to my own work.

This website

The site explains what Counter Insights does and lets you send me a message. I do not use analytics cookies or advertising cookies. I do not sell personal data.

The site is hosted by Netlify, Inc. Like most hosts, Netlify may keep ordinary server logs – for example an IP address, browser type and the page requested – so the site can be delivered and protected. Netlify describes its own handling in its privacy notice.

The contact form

If you use the form on the contact page, I collect:

  • your name
  • your business name
  • your business type
  • your email address
  • your phone number, if you choose to give one
  • your message
  • whether you asked for the free margin check-up

I use this to reply, to arrange a free chat or margin check-up if you asked for one, and to prepare a tailored quote if you want to go further.

Please do not send card numbers. Please do not put sensitive information, such as health information, in the message unless you really need to.

Why I am allowed to use it

UK GDPR needs a lawful basis. I rely on:

  • Steps towards a contract (Article 6(1)(b)). You have asked me to contact you about possible work, a quote, or the free margin check-up.
  • Legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)). I have an interest in answering business enquiries and keeping a short record of what was asked, so I can follow up properly. I have balanced that against your rights. You can object, and I will stop unless I have a compelling reason to continue.

The tick box for the free margin check-up is a request for that conversation. It is not a sign-up to a mailing list. I will not add you to marketing emails from it.

How the form reaches me

The form is handled by my website host, Netlify. Netlify stores the submission and can email it to me. I read messages at liamadams24562@gmail.com, so the email provider that hosts that inbox also handles the message in order to deliver it.

Netlify is based in the United States. Where personal data is processed there, it is protected by the safeguards Netlify publishes for international transfers. Those are described in Netlify’s privacy notice.

How long I keep form messages

I keep them only so I can reply and follow up. If we do not work together, I delete them within 12 months of our last contact. If we do work together, the next section applies to the data from that work.

Data from client work

If we work together, I only ask for the data I need. Your figures are commercially sensitive, and rotas, loyalty and booking data may include personal data about staff and customers. I work in a GDPR-aware way as standard:

  • I work from standard exports of your till or EPOS and supplier invoices, and from other records we agree, such as rotas, timesheets, or Apicbase exports
  • I use summary or pseudonymised data wherever possible
  • I keep files in encrypted storage protected by multi-factor authentication
  • I never take card numbers
  • I delete your data when our work ends, or whenever you ask
  • We put scope, confidentiality and data handling in a written agreement

For that work I rely on contract (Article 6(1)(b)), because you have asked me to do it. Where a business record happens to contain limited personal data – a staff rota, for example – I also rely on legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)) and I use the least data I can. I do not pass your data to other businesses.

Your rights

You can ask me to:

  • tell you whether I hold personal data about you, and for a copy (access)
  • correct it (rectification)
  • delete it (erasure)
  • limit how I use it (restriction)
  • provide a portable copy, where that right applies (data portability)
  • stop processing that is based on legitimate interests (objection)

I will respond within one month. I may need to confirm it is you before I do.

You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO), the UK regulator for personal data: ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint, or call the ICO on 0303 123 1113. I would like the chance to put things right first.

Changes to this notice

If I change this notice, I will update the date at the top of the page.
