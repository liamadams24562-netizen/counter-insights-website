No obligation

A free margin check-up.

A no-obligation look at your numbers, and a free chat about what they are telling you.

You already collect the data: till and POS sales, stock, supplier invoices, rotas. Large chains have analysts for this. This is a first conversation for independents – restaurants, cafés, shops and petrol stations.

What it is

  • A free chat, by phone, in person across North West London, or remotely anywhere in the UK
  • A look at the kind of numbers you already have, and where stock, food cost, menu profitability, rotas or sales might be worth a closer look
  • No new software, and nothing to install
  • No obligation to go any further

What it is not

It is not the Business Health Check. That is a separate, in-depth review with a written report and a one-hour walkthrough. On the call we can talk about whether that, a focused project, or a monthly retainer would help. I quote for the work. There is no price list.

You do not need to prepare a pack. If you already have a question about margins, stock or the rota, bring it. If you don’t, we can still talk.
